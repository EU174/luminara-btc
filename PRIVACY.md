# Privacy principles

Luminara BTC is being designed around data minimization.

Planned principles:

- anonymous access to public educational materials;
- no collection of seed phrases, private keys, balances, xpubs, UTXO history, or proof of funds;
- no collection of a learner's threat story, organization, or exact location for course routing;
- wallet addresses treated as pseudonymous personal data and masked outside privileged workflows;
- no raw IP addresses, wallet addresses, names, or personal risk profiles in learning analytics;
- privacy-safe aggregate learning outcomes rather than individual surveillance;
- account export and deletion limited to the separate Luminara BTC data boundary.

This file is a design statement, not yet a production privacy notice. A legal operator, contact,
retention schedule, and jurisdiction-specific notice must be approved before launch.
