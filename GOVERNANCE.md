# Governance

Luminara BTC is currently stewarded by its founding maintainers while nonprofit governance and
external review arrangements are being defined.

Planned controls include:

- reviewed pull requests and required CI for protected branches;
- two-person approval for sensitive administrator and recovery operations;
- documented maintainer access review and offboarding;
- transparent roadmap, changelog, releases, and material architecture decisions;
- independent review for authentication, wallet proof, privacy, and database isolation;
- no fabricated contributors, commits, users, or impact metrics.

Named roles, legal operator, licensing authority, and conflict-resolution procedures will be added
after formal approval.
